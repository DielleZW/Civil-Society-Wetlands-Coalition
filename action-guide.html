Submit a wetland report ↗

The form opens in Google Forms. Your report is only submitted after you complete and send it there. If damage is ongoing, contact EMA and seek urgent legal assistance in parallel.

Only share sensitive personal information and evidence if you are comfortable with the form's collection settings. Do not publish confidential locations or identifying details publicly.

Evidence to collect

Keep originals, dates and copies of communications.

Legal note: Possible routes include an EMA enforcement complaint, an Environmental Protection Order where applicable, consideration of section 104 remedies, a criminal complaint where an offence is supported by evidence, an appeal under section 130, and urgent court relief. Deadlines, standing and remedies require Zimbabwean legal advice. Not every project automatically requires an EIA, and inability to show a certificate does not itself prove illegality.
